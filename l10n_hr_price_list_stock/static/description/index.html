Public Price List: Stock per Premises

Croatia · Government decisions NN 101/26 · Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise

Availability (dostupno / nedostupno) in the public price list of each premises from the stock of its own warehouse, as the rules require.

Hrvatski: raspoloživost u javnom cjeniku iz skladišta pojedinog objekta. Instalira se sam uz aplikaciju Skladište; s jednim skladištem ne treba ništa postavljati.

How it works

  • An add-on of Public Price List for Consumers (l10n_hr_price_list), installed automatically when Inventory is installed.
  • With several warehouses (Inventory → Configuration → Settings → Warehouses), each premises gets a Warehouse field: goods that track stock are available only when that warehouse has them.
  • Empty: the stock of the whole company. Goods without stock tracking are always available; services have no availability.
